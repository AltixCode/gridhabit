## Description

```
GridHabit turns your habits into a picture.

Every day you complete a habit fills in a square. Miss a day and you see the gap.
After a few weeks you are not reading a number — you are looking at the shape of
your own consistency, and you do not want to break it.

THE GRID
Each habit gets a GitHub-style contribution grid. A year of effort on one screen.
The longer your run, the deeper the colour, so momentum is something you can
actually see.

BUILT TO BE OPENED FOR FIVE SECONDS
Open, tap, done. No feeds, no streaks-as-a-social-network, no nagging. Today's
habits are the first thing you see and one tap closes them out.

FLEXIBLE, NOT FUSSY
- Every day, a number of times a week, or specific weekdays
- A quiet reminder at the time you choose
- Twelve colours and a clean icon set
- Tap any past day to fix a missed check-in

YOURS, AND PRIVATE
Everything lives on your device in a local database. No account, no sign-up, no
servers. We cannot read your habits because we never receive them.

FREE AND PRO
Free: up to four habits, with a single small banner ad.
GridHabit Pro: unlimited habits, no ads ever, and data export. Available as a
one-time lifetime purchase — buy it once and it is yours, including whatever we
add next.
A yearly and monthly option is available if you prefer.

Designed for people who have tried the other habit trackers and wanted something
quieter.
```

## Positioning note

The category is crowded (Streaks, Habitify, Way of Life, HabitKit). Do not
compete on feature count. The whole pitch is *one visualisation, done well, in a
quiet app*. Every piece of copy above leads with the grid.
