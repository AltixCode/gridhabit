# GridHabit Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 13 September 2026**

GridHabit is built by AltixCode. This policy explains what the app does and does
not do with your information. It is short because the app does very little.

## Your habit data stays on your device

Your habits — their names, colours, schedules and every day you have checked
off — are stored in a database on your phone. They are not uploaded, not backed
up to our servers, and not visible to us. There is no account and no sign-up.
We could not read your habits if we wanted to, because we never receive them.

If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it. You can also erase it from
inside the app at any time.

## Advertising

The free version of GridHabit shows a single banner advertisement, served by
Google AdMob. To serve and measure those ads, Google receives:

- your device's advertising identifier,
- basic device and app information,
- an approximate location derived from your IP address.

Google's handling of that information is governed by the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

On iOS, you are asked whether GridHabit may track you across apps before any
personalised ad is requested. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not
personalised. You can change this later in *Settings → Privacy & Security →
Tracking*. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in
*Settings → Google → Ads*.

**Purchasing GridHabit Pro removes all advertising, and with it all advertising
data collection.**

## Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We never see your payment details.
We use [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) to verify that a
purchase is valid and to keep your Pro status working across your devices.
RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier generated for your installation and
the purchase receipt from the store. It does not receive your habit data.

## Notifications

If you turn on a reminder, it is scheduled by your device's operating system.
The reminder never leaves your phone and no push server is involved.

## Children

GridHabit is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect
information from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published here
and the date above will change.
